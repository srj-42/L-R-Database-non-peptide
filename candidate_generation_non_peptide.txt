------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT 2 — CANDIDATE GENERATION  (secondary; produces leads, never verdicts)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use only to propose pairs for checking. Anything this prompt returns enters the database as
a CANDIDATE at 'pending' and leaves that state through Prompt 1, whose answers are
written to 'confirmed' once the load gates pass (no curator read required since 2026-10-09).

system_content:

You are a biomedical research assistant. For a given endogenous small molecule, list the
human proteins reported to be its direct receptors or sensors, using real-time search.

Rules:

1. Output official HGNC gene symbols only.
2. Separate symbols with commas. No other text.
3. Include only proteins reported to BIND the molecule or to be DIRECTLY ACTIVATED or
   INHIBITED by it. Exclude enzymes that make or break it and transporters that move it,
   unless that protein is also reported to signal in response to it.
4. Exclude proteins linked to the molecule only by co-expression, pathway membership or
   text-mining.
5. Where a receptor is a complex, list the subunits reported to contact the molecule.

user_content:

For {chemical_name} ({identifier_type}: {identifier}), list up to 15 human receptors that
directly bind it or are directly activated or inhibited by it. If fewer than 15 are
reported, list only those.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES FOR WHOEVER RUNS THESE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Always fill {identifier} — HMDB where the chemical has one, otherwise PubChem CID, ChEBI
  or InChIKey. 590 of the 689 chemicals in this build carry an HMDB id; the remaining 99 are
  name-keyed only and are exactly the ones most likely to be confused with a near neighbour.

- Fill {synonyms} from the chemical's recorded synonym list, and include the Greek-letter
  form if one exists. No source in this build publishes Greek-letter spellings, but the
  primary literature uses them throughout.

- A returned PMID is written straight to 'confirmed' once the mechanical gates pass: the
  PMID resolves, the paper is not a review or retracted, the quoted sentence is verbatim in
  the genuine source (abstract, or real publisher full text), and the molecule and receptor
  names both occur in that text. No separate human read is required. (Changed 2026-10-09 at
  the project owner's instruction; before that a curator read was mandatory.)
  'confirmed' therefore now means "machine-verified against the source text", not "a person
  read the paper". extraction_method='llm_litsearch_np_prompt' records which rows these are.
  The verbatim gate is the load step's own check and is not optional - it is what stops the
  2026-09-03 self-referential-evidence incident recurring (PLAN.md invariants 8-12).

- Expect a high rejection rate and treat it as the prompt working. On the first batch of
  chemical-half citations checked by hand, 118 of 225 rows rested on a single tool paper and
  106 more could not be settled from the abstract; one row in 225 survived as a confirmation.
